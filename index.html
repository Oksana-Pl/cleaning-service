05 / Good to know

A few
fresh answers.

Still have a question?
Let’s talk ↗

How much does cleaning cost?+

Quotes depend on the service, size, quantity, condition and location. Share your details so we can discuss the right scope for your enquiry.

How long does drying take?+

Drying varies with the material, cleaning method, ventilation and room conditions. Ask about expected drying times when arranging your service.

Can every stain be removed?+

Some stains may be permanent. The material, age and type of stain affect the outcome. Discuss any marks when requesting your quote.

Can we arrange regular hotel laundry?+

Send your linen types, typical volumes and required frequency to discuss an ongoing arrangement for your property.

Do you cover my area?+

Include your town or Eircode in your enquiry. Service coverage will need to be confirmed for your location.
